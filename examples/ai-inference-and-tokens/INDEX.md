# AI inference and tokens

## Why This Matters

This is common AI vocabulary that many people see before they have a clear mental model.
It is a useful public example because a short first pass is enough to be helpful, while
also leaving natural next layers for a later revisit.

## Current Handle

Inference is when a trained AI model is being used: the model takes new input and produces
an output. Tokens are the small chunks of text the model reads and writes. Practical mental
model: **inference is the AI doing the work; tokens are the pieces of text it works with**.
